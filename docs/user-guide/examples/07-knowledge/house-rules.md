# House rules for 12 Linden Street

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours apply on weekdays from 1 pm to 3 pm and every night from 10 pm to 6 am. On Sundays and
public holidays, quiet hours apply all day.

## Rubbish

General waste is collected on Tuesdays; paper and recycling are collected alternately on Thursdays.
Report bulky waste to the property management; it may be put out on the street no earlier than the
evening before collection.

## Bicycles

Bicycles belong in the bicycle cellar. No bicycles or prams may be left in the stairwell, because it
is an escape route.
